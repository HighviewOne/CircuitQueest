Built for engineers who learn by doing.

Quest progression

Pass a quest's quiz to unlock the next one. No skipping — the math compounds.

Multiple-choice and numeric input with tolerance. Get a wrong answer? See exactly why.

◐

42 topics, 9 tracks

From Ohm's Law to ML hardware. Deep enough for ECE coursework, friendly enough for tinkerers.

◌

Offline-first

Every lesson, quiz, and progress entry lives on your device. No internet permission, no accounts, no ads.

↑

XP you actually earn

+50 XP per lesson, +100 the first time you pass a quiz, and +10 for every point you beat your best score. Retaking an easy quiz earns nothing.
